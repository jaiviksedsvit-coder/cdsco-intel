CDSCO Regulatory Classification & Terminology Guide

Official Indian regulatory taxonomy, lineage distinctions, and badging rules
★ Group A: First in India Innovator NCE / NBE

First Clearance of a New Molecular Entity

The very first time CDSCO granted clearance for this active molecular substance in India. Represents novel innovator drugs (New Chemical Entities or New Biological Entities) introducing new therapeutic mechanisms to the country.

Regulatory Basis: New Drug Clearance (Form 45 / Form 46) under New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules (NDCT 2019).
Example: First approval of Semaglutide or first novel targeted oncology antibody.
🧬 Group B: Biosimilar Similar Biologic

Follow-on Biologics in India

Biologics approved under India's Guidelines on Similar Biologics. These are follow-on biologics with demonstrated similarity in physicochemical structure, safety, and efficacy to an already approved innovator reference biologic.

Regulatory Basis: Approved with extensive comparative quality and clinical comparability characterization.
Example: Biosimilar Trastuzumab, Adalimumab, Rituximab, Bevacizumab.
💊 Group C: Generic Small Molecule Generic

Bioequivalent Small Molecule Copies

Chemically synthesized bioequivalent formulations containing the identical active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) in the same dosage form and strength as the innovator reference product.

Regulatory Basis: Subsequent clearance backed by bioequivalence (BE) and dissolution profiling.
Example: Generic Vildagliptin 50mg tablets or generic Apixaban filings.
Line Ext · First: YYYY Lifecycle Management

Subsequent Dosages & Formulations

A subsequent regulatory approval (new dosage strength, new vial presentation, extended-release formulation, or pediatric dosage) for a molecule that was already previously cleared in an earlier calendar year in India.

Badge Reading: "First: 2020" signifies the active molecule was first introduced in 2020, and this filing is a later addition.
Example: New pre-filled pen or sustained-release tablet for an existing molecule.
🧬 Biologic 💊 Small Mol
Molecular Classification

Macromolecules vs Chemical Entities

Biologics: Large, complex macromolecules (monoclonal antibodies, fusion proteins, vaccines, peptides) produced in living cell expression systems.
Small Molecules: Low-molecular-weight chemical entities (< 900 Da) synthesized by organic chemistry, typically administered orally.

Examples: Biologic: Durvalumab, Ustekinumab. Small Molecule: Vildagliptin, SGLT2 inhibitors.
Single 🧩 FDC Combo
Formulation Structure

Single Active vs Fixed-Dose Combination

Monotherapy (Single): Contains exactly one active pharmaceutical ingredient.
Combination (FDC): Fixed-Dose Combination containing 2 or more active pharmaceutical ingredients combined in a single finished dosage form.

Examples: Single: Vildagliptin 50mg. FDC: Vildagliptin + Metformin Hydrochloride tablets.
